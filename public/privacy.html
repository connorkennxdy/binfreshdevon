Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

Bin Fresh Devon provides wheelie bin cleaning in North Devon. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, email info@binfreshdevon.com or call 07394 536053.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry through our website we collect your name, email address, postcode, and, if you provide them, your phone number, number of bins and message. When you book a clean, Square Appointments collects your name, contact details, address and payment card details on our behalf.

How we use it

We use your details to reply to enquiries, carry out and schedule your cleans, take payment for the service you have booked, and contact you about your bookings. We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Who we share it with

We use Square to manage bookings and payments, and Netlify to host this website and receive enquiry form submissions. These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy and security terms. Card details are handled by Square and are never stored by us.

How long we keep it

We keep customer records for as long as you use our service and for up to six years afterwards for accounting purposes. Enquiries that do not lead to a booking are deleted within 12 months.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email us and we will respond within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
