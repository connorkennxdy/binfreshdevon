Terms of service

Last updated: November 2026

Our service

Bin Fresh Devon cleans, sanitises and deodorises wheelie bins at your property in North Devon. Plans include a clean every 4 weeks for the number of bins you choose when booking.

Booking and payment

You book and pay online at binfreshdevon.co.uk/book. Cleans take place Monday to Saturday in the time window you choose. Card payments are processed by Stripe.

For a one-off clean you pay in full when you book. For a 4-weekly plan you pay for your first clean when you book; your card is then saved and charged automatically at the plan price on the morning of each following clean, every 4 weeks. Prices include everything listed on our website for that service. If a regular payment fails, we will let you know and may hold the clean until it is paid.

If we ever change a plan price, we will email you at least 14 days before the new price applies.

Access to your bins

Please leave your bins out and accessible on your chosen day, ideally after they have been emptied. If we cannot reach your bins, or they still contain waste, we may not be able to clean them on that visit.

Cancelling and rescheduling

There is no minimum contract. Use the manage link in your booking email or reminder text, or contact us.

  • You can cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 24 hours before your visit.
  • Within 24 hours of your visit, the visit is charged in full.
  • If we can't clean (for example because of weather, access, or bins that are blocked or still full), we'll move you to the next available day or refund the visit.

Cancelling a 4-weekly plan stops all further charges. If you cancel more than 24 hours before a clean you have already paid for, you can choose to cancel that clean too and be refunded.

Commercial customers

Commercial cleaning is quoted per site. Frequency, price and access arrangements are agreed in writing before work starts.
