Terms of service

Last updated: October 2026

Our service

Bin Fresh Devon cleans, sanitises and deodorises wheelie bins at your property in North Devon. Plans include a clean every 4 weeks for the number of bins you choose when booking.

Booking and payment

Bookings are made through Square Appointments. Your card is saved when you book and charged after each clean at the price shown for your plan. Prices include everything listed on our website for that plan.

Access to your bins

Please leave your bins out and accessible on your chosen day, ideally after they have been emptied. If we cannot reach your bins, or they still contain waste, we may not be able to clean them on that visit.

Pausing and cancelling

There is no minimum contract. You can pause or cancel your plan at any time by contacting us before your next scheduled clean.

Commercial customers

Commercial cleaning is quoted per site. Frequency, price and access arrangements are agreed in writing before work starts.

Contact

For any questions about these terms, email info@binfreshdevon.com or call 07394 536053.
